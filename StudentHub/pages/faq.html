Help Center & Knowledge Base

How Can We Help You Today?

Explore instant answers for portal login, assignment submissions, attendance tracking, and academic support.

How do I reset or change my StudentHub password?
Go to the Login Page and click on "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered student email address to receive a secure 6-digit reset code. If you are already logged in, you can also change your password under your Student Profile settings.
How is attendance calculated and updated?
Faculty members mark attendance daily. Your overall percentage is calculated automatically: (Lectures Attended / Total Conducted Lectures) * 100. A minimum of 75% attendance is required to appear in semester examinations. Check your current standing on the Attendance Page.
What are the guidelines for uploading assignment solutions?
Assignments can be uploaded directly on the Assignments Page. Acceptable file formats include PDF, ZIP, and DOCX (up to 25MB). Make sure to upload your file prior to the posted deadline.
Where can I download subject study materials and PDFs?
Navigate to the Materials Page to download lecture slides, lab manuals, syllabus PDFs, and previous question papers uploaded by faculty and top students.
How does the Light/Dark theme switcher work?
Click the Theme Toggle Switch (Sun/Moon icon) at the top right of the navigation header on any page. StudentHub uses HTML5 localStorage to store your preference, ensuring dark mode remains enabled across all pages.
How do I use the responsive mobile navigation drawer?
On mobile screens and tablets, click the animated 3-bar hamburger icon in the top right. The mobile menu drawer slides down smoothly with all site navigation links. Clicking outside or selecting a link closes the menu automatically.
How do I report a bug or contact academic support?
Visit our Contact Support Page to submit an online message, or email our IT helpdesk directly at studenthub@gmail.com.
