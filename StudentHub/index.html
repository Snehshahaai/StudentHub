Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about StudentHub? Find answers to common queries about login, attendance calculation, assignment submission, and technical support.

How do I reset my StudentHub portal password?
If you forgot your password, go to the Login Page and click on "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered student email address to receive a secure password reset link. Alternatively, contact your department administrator.
How is attendance calculated on StudentHub?
Attendance is calculated based on total lectures conducted versus total lectures attended. Minimum required attendance is 75%. You can view real-time percentage progress bars on your Attendance Dashboard.
What file formats are supported for assignment submissions?
StudentHub supports PDF, ZIP, DOCX, and common source code files (.java, .py, .cpp, .html). File sizes must not exceed 25MB per assignment submission.
How do I enable Dark Mode on StudentHub?
Click the Theme Toggle Button (Sun/Moon icon) located at the top right of the navigation menu. Your preferred theme preference will be remembered across all pages automatically.
Who should I contact for technical issues or portal errors?
You can send a message directly via our Contact Page or send an email to studenthub@gmail.com with details of the issue.
